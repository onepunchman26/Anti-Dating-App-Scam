# Owner review / 所有者审阅

## English

Already settled: root Desktop entry; approved notes scope; use of the ChatGPT plan
with extra credits disabled. These do not require another design approval. Work is
manually triggered, and the hourly automation remains paused.

After the final build, open **AI-SlowMatch.exe** and check:

1. **Voice:** connect/select a microphone, choose English or Chinese, record a short
   fictional sentence, review the draft and send it manually. Physical capture and
   spoken Chinese still require your actual device; synthetic recognition is not equivalent.
2. **Usefulness:** try a short chat, a correction and a topic change; End; inspect the
   reflection's original quotes and tentative wording. Verify that Back works.
3. **Control:** add one harmless approved note, correct it, pause reuse and delete it.
   Local saved history and notes are unencrypted. Use fictional content until that
   storage choice and the model's behavior meet your expectations.
4. **Private exchange:** use two fictional summaries first. Review the encrypted
   attachment workflow; decide personally whether to share anything. Email delivery
   and any third party's consent are yours to perform, not automated acceptance steps.

No payment, public deployment, real-data import or external message is awaiting approval.
If broader encrypted local storage is desired, it is a separate future design decision.

## 中文版

已确定的事项：根目录桌面入口、已批准记忆的范围，以及关闭额外积分后使用 ChatGPT 套餐。
这些不需要再次设计审批。工作由人工触发，小时自动任务保持暂停。

最终构建后，打开 **AI-SlowMatch.exe**，检查：

1. **语音：**连接／选择麦克风，选择中或英文，录一句虚构内容，核对草稿后自行发送。
   实体录音和中文口述仍需你的实际设备，合成语音识别不能替代这一项。
2. **实用性：**短聊几句，纠正一次并换话题；结束后核对画像原文引用与暂定措辞，同时检查返回。
3. **控制权：**批准一条无敏感内容的记忆，纠正、暂停复用并删除。本地历史与记忆未加密；
   在确认存储方式与模型行为符合预期前，请使用虚构内容。
4. **私密交换：**先用双方虚构摘要检查加密附件流程。是否分享由你亲自决定；邮件发送及第三方
   同意由你自行完成，不是自动验收步骤。

目前没有需要批准的付款、公开部署、真实资料导入或外部发消息事项。
如希望进一步加密整个本地存储，应另作后续设计决定。

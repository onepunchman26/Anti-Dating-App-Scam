### Your important checks

1. **ChatGPT allowance and real AI quality.** Open ChatGPT Usage settings and
   verify that extra credits are disabled for this app before checking the
   confirmation in AI-SlowMatch. The app cannot verify that remote switch itself.
   The actual account screen and model discovery have been observed successfully,
   including GPT-6-Astra, GPT-5.6-Sol, GPT-5.6-Terra, GPT-5.6-Luna and GPT-5.5.
   The proposed default is GPT-5.6-Luna, chosen from that returned catalog. Its

   been measured. This observation did not involve a model request. If you choose
   to test real chat, start with fictional answers and review each outgoing
   request. Check whether questions are useful, Start/End is clear, reflections
   use your exact words fairly, both translations preserve the meaning, and
   private comparisons keep uncertainty. A login badge or passing synthetic test
   does not establish real-model quality.
2. **Microphone and optional offline speech.** No microphone input device was
   detected in the current check. Connect or enable one if you want voice input;
   you can keep typing otherwise. The app offers separate English and Simplified
   Chinese offline models. Download the free official model only if the selected
   language is not already installed. Press Start recording yourself; each clip
   is limited to 30 seconds. Stop, inspect and edit the recognized text, then
   choose Use this text to add it to your draft. Press Send separately when you
   want to send the edited message. Transcription must not send automatically.
   The English recognizer processed synthetic audio successfully; the Chinese
   model was loaded, but Chinese transcription quality and actual microphone
   capture have not yet been demonstrated. These checks are not evidence of
   recognition accuracy for your voice, device or room.
3. **Private sharing with both participants' permission.** Choose one separately
   saved reflection and review the complete summary that will leave the app.
   The selected package excludes the raw conversation, original quotations,
   account credentials and saved history; its narrative may still contain
   personal information. Export requires confirmation and a password or
   passphrase of at least 12 characters. If you want to send the encrypted
   attachment by email, send it yourself and communicate the password through
   another channel. The app and agent do not send it for you. Confirm the other
   participant permits this private use before selecting and opening their file.
   Review both summaries and the exact AI request before an online comparison.
   Check that the result presents discussion points, boundaries, tensions and
   unknowns without scores, rankings or a final decision about whether to date.
   Encryption does not verify identity or make an AI summary true. Do not forward

4. **Ease of use and wording.** Try the primary path without configuring advanced
   prompts or providers. Check the model picker, visible success state, portrait

   whether the app feels like a helpful relationship copilot, whether it is easy
   to stop, and whether its wording preserves your choices and avoids turning

### 你需要核对的重要事项

1. **ChatGPT 额度与真实 AI 质量。** 先打开 ChatGPT 用量设置，核对本应用的额外积分
   已关闭，再在 AI-SlowMatch 勾选确认。本应用无法自行验证远端开关。实际账号页面及
   模型发现已经观察到成功，包括 GPT-6-Astra、GPT-5.6-Sol、GPT-5.6-Terra、
   GPT-5.6-Luna 和 GPT-5.5。建议默认项为从此账号返回目录中选出的 GPT-5.6-Luna，
   但其速度、额度消耗及画像结构化输出兼容性尚未测量。此观察没有调用模型。如果你选择
   测试真实聊天，先使用虚构回答，逐次审阅发送内容。检查提问是否有帮助，开始／结束是否
   清楚，画像是否公平使用你的原话，两种翻译是否保持意思，以及私下比较是否保留不确定性。
   登录成功标记或合成测试通过，都不能证明真实模型质量。
2. **麦克风与可选本地语音。** 当前检查未发现麦克风输入设备。如果想使用语音，请连接
   或启用麦克风；也可以继续打字。应用分别提供英语与简体中文离线模型，只有所选语言尚未
   安装时，才需要下载免费的官方模型。录音由你亲自点击开始，每段最多 30 秒。停止后核对
   并修改识别文字，选择“使用这些文字”只会加入草稿；准备好后再单独点击发送。转写不得
   自动发送。英语识别器已经成功处理合成音频；中文模型已经加载，但中文转写质量与真实
   麦克风录音尚未验证。这些检查不能证明对你的声音、设备或房间的识别准确性。
3. **双方许可下的私密交换。** 选择一份已经独立保存的画像，审阅完整的外发摘要。

   AI 摘要成为事实。没有发送者进一步许可，请勿转发资料包。
4. **易用性与措辞。** 不配置高级提示词或提供方，直接试用主流程。检查模型选择框、

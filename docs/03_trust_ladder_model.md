# Trust Ladder Model

The trust ladder is a pacing model. It does not decide who is good or bad. It helps users avoid moving faster than available evidence supports.

## Stages

| Stage | Name | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | UNKNOWN_STRANGER | A person with little verified context. |
| 1 | LOW_PRESSURE_CHAT | Conversation stays low-pressure and platform safety tools remain available. |
| 2 | REPEATED_CONSISTENT_INTERACTION | Interaction is repeated, ordinary, and not built on urgency. |
| 3 | BOUNDARY_RESPECT_VERIFIED | Identity has been checked through safe, consent-based methods and boundaries are respected. |
| 4 | SAFETY_CHECKED_OFFLINE_MEETING | Offline meeting planning includes a public place, independent transport, and outside awareness. |
| 5 | DEEPER_RELATIONSHIP_EXPLORATION | Deeper relationship discussion happens slowly and mutually. |

## Allowed Actions

- Stay at the current stage when information is limited.
- Slow down when pressure, inconsistency, or verification avoidance appears.
- Step back when high-risk signals appear.
- Gather more information through safe, consent-based methods.
- Use a safety checklist before any offline meeting.

## Disallowed Escalations

- Do not recommend rapid intimacy or commitment.
- Do not treat AI output as proof of safety.
- Do not recommend secret investigations, hidden tracking, or private data gathering.
- Do not move toward offline meeting when money, coercion, or identity pressure appears.
- Do not convert the stage into a public score.

## Examples

### Low-Pressure Chat

A user has exchanged several normal messages. No money, urgency, or boundary pressure appears. The recommendation is to stay at low-pressure chat or continue slowly.

### Boundary Warning

A user says they declined a video call and the other person kept pressuring them. The recommendation is to slow down, stay at the current stage, and gather more information.

### High-Risk Signal

A user says an online-only contact asked for crypto investment funds. The recommendation is to stop financial interaction, step back, and use platform or professional support if needed.

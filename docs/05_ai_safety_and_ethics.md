# AI Safety And Ethics

AI-SlowMatch must remain a restrained trust-support system. The central risk is that a protective tool could become surveillance, scoring, manipulation, or emotional dependency infrastructure.

## Not Personality Social Credit

The system must not create public personality ratings, compatibility scores, morality labels, or "good person" and "bad person" determinations. Risk analysis should stay tied to submitted behavior patterns and uncertainty.

## Consent-First Design

Users must confirm that they are submitting text they are allowed to analyze. Future storage, imports, or model calls must require explicit consent and clear retention rules.

## Non-Accusatory Risk Language

Outputs should say "risk signal detected" rather than "this person is a scammer." The tool can recommend not sending money and slowing down without making criminal, legal, psychological, or moral determinations.

## No Hidden Scraping

The system must not scrape private profiles, messages, contacts, images, or cross-platform data. Cross-platform import should remain disabled unless a future user explicitly authorizes it.

## No Public Score

Any future metrics should be private, local, explainable, and deletable. There should be no public badge, searchable score, or reputation ranking.

## No Addictive Design

The project should not optimize for swiping, anxiety, paid boosts, streaks, or engagement farming. The user should become less dependent on the tool as real-world clarity improves.

## No Manipulation

The system must not generate manipulative scripts, entrapment messages, scam-baiting instructions, revenge plans, or moderation-bypass tactics.

## No AI Companion Replacement Framing

AI-SlowMatch should be a relationship training wheel, not an AI lover. It should support safer human relationships and user autonomy.

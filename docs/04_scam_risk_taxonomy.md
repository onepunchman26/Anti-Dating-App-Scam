# Scam Risk Taxonomy

AI-SlowMatch uses risk signals, not accusations. A signal means "this pattern deserves caution." It does not prove intent, identity, or wrongdoing.

## Romance Scam Patterns

- rapid emotional escalation;
- online-only intimacy;
- emergency stories;
- money, gift card, crypto, or wire requests;
- guilt or fear pressure;
- isolation from friends and family;
- refusal to verify identity through safe, consent-based methods.

## Dating App Scam Patterns

- immediate pressure to leave the platform;
- fake profile details;
- inconsistent biography;
- requests for private images;
- requests for identity documents or financial information;
- threats or extortion after private image sharing.

## Chatbot Or Fake Profile Patterns

- generic replies that ignore context;
- repeated refusal to video call or meet safely;
- profile details that change across messages;
- scripted emotional escalation;
- unusual links, downloads, or external account requests.

## Crypto And Investment Romance Scam Pattern

Often called "pig butchering," this pattern links emotional trust to investment pressure:

1. Establish friendly or romantic contact.
2. Move off-platform.
3. Build daily intimacy.
4. Introduce crypto, forex, mining, or trading.
5. Show fake profits.
6. Pressure larger deposits.
7. Block withdrawal or demand fees.

## Risk Signal Severity Table

| Signal | Severity | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Money, gift card, crypto, wire, or bank details request | High | Especially high-risk before in-person trust exists. |
| Investment opportunity tied to romance | High | Treat as high-risk even if emotionally warm. |
| Private images or sensitive identity data request | High | Creates coercion and privacy risk. |
| Rapid emotional escalation | Medium | Not proof, but a common acceleration tactic. |
| Refusal to verify or meet safely | Medium | Raises uncertainty. |
| Immediate off-platform pressure | Medium | Reduces platform safety options. |
| Emergency story | Medium | Higher if paired with money request. |
| Isolation from friends or family | Medium | Weakens outside perspective. |
| Boundary violation | Medium | Trust should not progress while repeated. |

## Uncertainty Language

Use phrases such as:

- "risk signal detected";
- "insufficient information";
- "this does not prove malicious intent";
- "consider slowing down";
- "do not send money";
- "verify identity through safe, consent-based methods."

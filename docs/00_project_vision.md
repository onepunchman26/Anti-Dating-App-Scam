# Project Vision

## Current private loop (2026-09-30) — English

The owner wants a relationship copilot that reduces defensive calculation while preserving choice. Start and End stay under the user's control. Optional offline voice recognition inserts editable answers. A separately reviewed personal reflection leads to a minimal encrypted attachment; people exchange these themselves, then import both for a private AI discussion of possible common ground, tensions and questions. Risk support concerns concrete behavior, not a score or a final judgment of a person. There is no swiping catalogue or automatic contact. Local file decryption and an online ChatGPT request are distinct: both summaries need permission and exact disclosure before cloud analysis. See [Private exchange and voice](31_private_exchange_voice.md).

## 当前私下流程（2026-09-30）— 中文版

所有者希望恋爱军师能减轻防御性计算，同时保留自主选择。开始与结束由用户掌控，可选离线语音识别将回答加入可编辑草稿。个人画像经独立审阅后，形成最小加密附件；双方自行交换，再导入两份文件，请 AI 私下讨论可能的共同点、分歧与需要沟通的问题。风险支持针对具体行为，不给人打分或作最终判决。不提供互相挑选的目录或自动联系。本地文件解密与在线 ChatGPT 请求分开；云端分析前，双方摘要都需要许可及精确披露。详见[私密交换与语音](31_private_exchange_voice.md)。

## Current direction (2026-09-29)

The current local checkpoint opens with **AI Chat**, then user-controlled **Start** and **End**, with no fixed duration or market setup. Only a later, explicit action requests a provisional self-reflection; saving it requires another confirmation. The report cites this session's typed words and marks interpretations as low confidence. Risk signals and relationship fit remain separate; missing information is not a danger label. A single visible ChatGPT connection uses implemented official browser sign-in and separate plan authorization, tested only with synthetic fakes; real account, entitlement and model behavior remain unverified. The user must verify plan eligibility and disable extra-credit use for this app in ChatGPT settings, which the app cannot itself confirm. Population matching, feeds, scores, and public launch are outside this milestone. Earlier matching and infrastructure proposals below record previous design exploration. Full bilingual direction: [Conversation-first product](30_conversation_first_product.md).

## 当前方向（2026-09-29）

## Earlier vision and design exploration / 较早的愿景与设计探索

AI-SlowMatch is a prototype for anti-dating-scam and relationship-trust infrastructure. It is not a normal dating app, not a centralized matching oracle, and not an AI lover replacement.

**Note on the compatibility-matching addition (see `docs/11_compatibility_matching_plan.md`):** the project now also supports an opt-in, fully decentralized deep-compatibility comparison between two specific people who already know each other or are already in contact through other means. This does not contradict the line above — there is no central database of users, no discovery feed, and no algorithm searching a population to surface candidates. People still find each other entirely on their own. The feature only ever compares two locally-held profiles that both people explicitly chose to share with each other, and produces a non-scoring reflection (alignment/divergence points), never a ranking or percentage match. Treat any future implementation that drifts toward a centralized pool, swiping, or discovery feed as out of scope and a violation of this vision, not a natural extension of it.

## Social Atomization

Many people now seek intimacy after the weakening of everyday social institutions that once helped people meet, assess reputation, and receive informal guidance. Family networks, neighborhood ties, local associations, faith communities, workplaces, and friend-of-friend introductions still exist, but they are less reliable as broad trust infrastructure.

This produces a lonely but highly connected environment. People can contact strangers instantly while having fewer shared intermediaries to help interpret risk.

## Dating Platformization

Dating platforms reorganize intimacy through profiles, ranking, attention metrics, paid visibility, filters, and swiping. These mechanics can help discovery, but they also intensify information asymmetry. Users often know very little about each other, and platforms may optimize for activity rather than trust repair.

## Marketized Intimacy

When romantic life is framed mainly as a marketplace, people can begin to treat each other as interchangeable options. This can reward rapid filtering, defensive self-presentation, and instrumental interaction. Scam risk grows in the same environment because trust cues become thin and easy to fake.

## Gender Antagonism As Trust Deficit

AI-SlowMatch treats gender antagonism as a symptom of mutual risk perception rather than a moral explanation. Different groups experience different safety worries, rejection patterns, economic pressures, and reputational risks. The system should never use gender-hostile language or imply that scam risk belongs to one gender.

## Trust Spiral Decline Model

The working model is:

```text
social atomization
  -> weak relational intermediaries
  -> platformized intimacy
  -> marketized dating logic
  -> mutual suspicion and defensive filtering
  -> trust deficit
  -> scams, manipulation, and sincere-user exit
  -> deeper trust deficit
```

## AI-SlowMatch As Trust Infrastructure

The goal is not to automate love. The goal is to provide slow, consent-based decision support:

- detect possible scam patterns;
- explain uncertainty and missing information;
- support privacy-safe verification;
- encourage low-risk pacing;
- help users notice repeated patterns over time;
- keep final decisions with the user.

## AI As Relationship Training Wheel

AI-SlowMatch should help users re-enter real relationships safely. It should not become a substitute partner, an emotional dependency loop, or a system that extracts more intimate data to keep users engaged.

## 扩展愿景与"双线平衡"（2026-07 补充）

作者提出了一个比"反诈骗约会工具"更大的愿景：以"去标签化的长期动态人格建模"为内核，先在约会/反诈骗切口验证，再纵深到教育、兴趣开发与自我实现，作为对社会原子化的更深层回应。完整论述见 `paper/产品愿景与双轨路线.md`，配套竞品与文献调研见 `paper/竞品与同类构想调研.md`。

这一扩展愿景涉及"大厂数据一键导入、大数据分析、群体匹配"等中心化能力，与本文上方强调的"无中心用户库、无发现流、隐私优先"原则**存在张力**。二者通过**"官服 / 私服"双线平衡**调和，而非相互取代：

- **私服（去中心化线）= 本文原始愿景的忠实延续**：隐私优先、数据留在用户侧、非评分、无中心池。本文所有红线在此线原样保留。
- **官服（资本主流线）= 一个受最重监管、显式标注为"高风险"的例外**：承认"要启动就需要中心化能力与大厂/政府"，但用实名可追责性、可验证身份的最小披露、以及"用户随时可迁出到私服"的可退出性来对冲风险。
- **开源内核 = 两线之间的宪法**：确保无论跑在哪条线，目标函数（不操控、抬高伪装成本、决定权留给用户）不可篡改。

**判定原则不变**：任何单一实现若漂向"中心化池 / 滑动 / 发现流"且**缺少可退出到私服的保证**，仍视为违背本愿景，而非其自然延伸。

# Project Vision

AI-SlowMatch is a prototype for anti-dating-scam and relationship-trust infrastructure. It is not a normal dating app, not a matching oracle, and not an AI lover replacement.

## Social Atomization

Many people now seek intimacy after the weakening of everyday social institutions that once helped people meet, assess reputation, and receive informal guidance. Family networks, neighborhood ties, local associations, faith communities, workplaces, and friend-of-friend introductions still exist, but they are less reliable as broad trust infrastructure.

This produces a lonely but highly connected environment. People can contact strangers instantly while having fewer shared intermediaries to help interpret risk.

## Dating Platformization

Dating platforms reorganize intimacy through profiles, ranking, attention metrics, paid visibility, filters, and swiping. These mechanics can help discovery, but they also intensify information asymmetry. Users often know very little about each other, and platforms may optimize for activity rather than trust repair.

## Marketized Intimacy

When romantic life is framed mainly as a marketplace, people can begin to treat each other as interchangeable options. This can reward rapid filtering, defensive self-presentation, and instrumental interaction. Scam risk grows in the same environment because trust cues become thin and easy to fake.

## Gender Antagonism As Trust Deficit

AI-SlowMatch treats gender antagonism as a symptom of mutual risk perception rather than a moral explanation. Different groups experience different safety worries, rejection patterns, economic pressures, and reputational risks. The system should never use gender-hostile language or imply that scam risk belongs to one gender.

## Trust Spiral Decline Model

The working model is:

```text
social atomization
  -> weak relational intermediaries
  -> platformized intimacy
  -> marketized dating logic
  -> mutual suspicion and defensive filtering
  -> trust deficit
  -> scams, manipulation, and sincere-user exit
  -> deeper trust deficit
```

## AI-SlowMatch As Trust Infrastructure

The goal is not to automate love. The goal is to provide slow, consent-based decision support:

- detect possible scam patterns;
- explain uncertainty and missing information;
- support privacy-safe verification;
- encourage low-risk pacing;
- help users notice repeated patterns over time;
- keep final decisions with the user.

## AI As Relationship Training Wheel

AI-SlowMatch should help users re-enter real relationships safely. It should not become a substitute partner, an emotional dependency loop, or a system that extracts more intimate data to keep users engaged.

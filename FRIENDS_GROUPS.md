# Friends and private circles / 好友与私人群组

## English

Open [Friends & groups](https://slowmatch-private-pilot.fizzydeer7.chatgpt.site/#friends)
on the existing private Site. Its current audience is still owner-only; a link does
not grant access. Real second- and third-account acceptance remains untested.

1. Sign in, choose a display name, confirm you are an adult, and choose whether
   people with your exact ID can find you. Copy your stable ID to someone you know.
   Looking up an ID reveals only a display name. A request needs the other person
   to accept. You need not publish a discovery summary or accept romantic matching.
2. Create a private group, invite accepted friends and wait for each to accept.
   Groups support up to eight accepted/pending members and expire within 30 days.
   Invitation links open the group; they confer no access. Removing/blocking a
   friend closes your common groups. Membership changes end current activities;
   create a new activity for the changed roster.
3. Select the 2–8 people participating in this activity. Romantic exploration is
   limited to two people and requires both people's separate opt-in. Every member
   provides only this activity's coarse areas, available time, travel limit, currency,
   budget, interests, exclusions and practical requirements. Review the exact audience
   and expiry before sharing. Private chats, reports and complete profiles stay local.
4. The service calculates all-member intersections. No overlap means no proposal;
   nobody's constraints are overridden by a majority. Travel time and venue suitability
   are currently manual checks, not verified map/distance calculations.
5. One willing member runs their own local assistant **0.8.2 or newer**, connects
   their authorized ChatGPT plan, selects a model and explicitly approves usage.
   Supply two public venue ideas and source links. This route does not search maps
   or verify venues automatically. Only the separately approved activity context is
   sent to OpenAI; the generator never reads private profiles or conversations.
   Every selected member must allow this shared AI processing. Others can participate
   through the Site without installing the assistant or completing an interview.
6. Review the generated draft, then approve sharing that exact version. Each person
   checks their own route, time, budget and requirements and confirms the same option.
   Changed conditions invalidate the proposal and confirmations. Withdrawal, expiry,
   leaving and blocking prevent later access/generation; previously received copies
   cannot be recalled. No booking, payment, calendar edit or external message occurs.

Language changes retain drafts and approvals and make no AI or publication request.
Names, submitted areas, currency and meeting instants remain as supplied. Installation
starts at the [original project's short guide](WEB_COMPANION.md), not a developer checkout.
Current verified results and remaining gates are in [CURRENT_STATUS.md](CURRENT_STATUS.md).

## 中文版

打开原私有网站的[好友与群组](https://slowmatch-private-pilot.fizzydeer7.chatgpt.site/#friends)。
目前仍仅限所有者访问，链接不会自动赋予权限。第二、第三个独立账号验收尚未实测。

1. 登录、填写昵称、确认成年，并选择是否允许知道完整 ID 的人查找你。把稳定 ID 告诉认识的人。
   查找仅显示昵称；发送申请后须对方自行接受。无需发布陌生人推荐摘要，也无需接受恋爱匹配。
2. 创建私人群组，邀请已确认好友，等待每个人接受。已加入和待处理邀请合计最多8人，群组期限
   最长30天。邀请链接只打开页面，不授予访问权限。删除／屏蔽好友会关闭双方共同群组。
   正式成员变化会结束当前活动，请为新名单重新创建活动。
3. 选择本次活动的2–8名参加者。恋爱探索仅限两人，须双方各自明确选择。每人仅填写此次活动的
   大致区域、可参加时间、路程上限、币种、预算、兴趣、不想参加的活动及实际安排要求。
   分享前审阅准确接收名单与期限。私人聊天、报告及完整档案继续留在本机。
4. 服务计算全员条件交集；没有交集就不生成方案，不以多数票覆盖个人条件。路程及场所是否适合
   目前须自行核对，不属于已核实的地图／距离计算。
5. 一位自愿成员启动自己的 **0.8.2 或更新版本**本机助手，连接已授权的 ChatGPT 套餐，选择模型，
   明确同意使用本人额度。填写两个公共场所方案与来源链接；当前路线不会自动查地图或核实场所。
   只有单独批准的活动上下文会发送给 OpenAI，生成器不读取私人档案或聊天。
   每位参加者须允许此次共享 AI 处理。其他人可以只用网站参与，无需安装助手或先完成访谈。
6. 审阅 AI 草案，再批准分享此准确版本。每个人核对自己的路线、时间、预算和必要条件，分别
   确认同一选项。修改条件使方案及确认失效。撤回、到期、退出和屏蔽阻止后续读取／生成，
   但无法收回已被接收的副本。不自动预订、付款、修改日历或向外发消息。

切换语言保留草稿和批准状态，不触发 AI 或发布。昵称、填写的区域、币种及见面时刻保留原值。
安装从[原项目简短指引](WEB_COMPANION.md)开始，无需开发者源码环境。
实测结果及剩余限制见[当前状态](CURRENT_STATUS.md)。

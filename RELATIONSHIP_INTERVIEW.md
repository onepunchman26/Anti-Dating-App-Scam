# Relationship-first conversations / 关系优先访谈

## English

Use the existing [SlowMatch page](https://slowmatch-private-pilot.fizzydeer7.chatgpt.site/#start)
with the [0.8.3 local assistant](https://github.com/onepunchman26/Anti-Dating-App-Scam/releases/tag/v0.8.3).
The local pairing, account authorization and private folder steps remain. Choose
Romantic relationships or Friendship, then start a dedicated conversation. Other
topics and older saved conversations keep their existing free conversation behavior.

The first question addresses the relationship you want. One reply can cover several
areas, so there is no mandatory questionnaire. The opening and clarifications count
toward at most eight initial questions. You can choose **View draft now**, pause, skip,
say you are unsure or have no experience at any time. Those choices do not imply a
negative trait. Quick-start 3–5 minutes and optional 10–20 minutes are design aims,
not required or measured completion times.

Draft items show the source wording and remain unconfirmed summaries of self-reports.
Correct the wording and approve it locally if useful. Removed/skipped/owner-reviewed
items cannot be silently replaced by AI. Removal affects the draft, not the saved raw
conversation. The original conversation remains in your selected private folder.
The draft is not proof of anyone's honesty, safety or personality.

Resume a saved conversation after reconnecting the same account/folder. To start a new
conversation using an earlier draft, select that same-topic draft explicitly. Its notes,
question count, skips and corrections carry over; its transcript does not. A fresh
conversation without that selection does not retrieve old drafts automatically. Sending
a message authorizes the bounded conversation and draft context to OpenAI; browsing,
editing, pausing or switching the interface language does not call AI.

When ready, **Explore further** lets you choose one relationship or life area. Mind,
Body, Spirit and Vocation are optional lenses for thinking, daily rhythm, belonging and
life direction. Completing them is never a condition of matching or joining a group.
The explicit next-message button calls AI using the current selected model and consents.

Saving an interview draft, confirming a private profile statement and publishing a
shared summary are separate actions. **Review for private profile** only copies your
selected reviewed wording into the existing private review editor; check and approve it
again there. Nothing is automatically copied into a public summary, friend/group report
or matching score. Friends and group invitees keep their lightweight participation path.

This project-authored method adaptation credits HUMAN 3.0, but does not install a
community Skill, copy its full prompt or perform its level assessment. See
[source credits](docs/39_open_source_credits.md) and [current validation](CURRENT_STATUS.md)
for what was actually checked and the remaining independent-account/platform gates.

## 中文版

打开原[慢慢相识网页](https://slowmatch-private-pilot.fizzydeer7.chatgpt.site/#start)，配合
[0.8.3本机助手](https://github.com/onepunchman26/Anti-Dating-App-Scam/releases/tag/v0.8.3)。
原有本机配对、账号授权与私人文件夹步骤保留。选择恋爱关系或朋友关系后开始专用会话。
其他主题与旧保存会话继续使用原来的自由对话方式。

首题先了解你期待的关系。一个回答可涵盖多个方面，不要求完成固定问卷。开场与澄清都计入
首轮最多八问。随时选择“先看草稿”、暂停、跳过、不确定或没有经验，不据此作负面人格判断。
三至五分钟快速开始、可选十至二十分钟对话只是设计目标，不是要求或已测得的完成时间。

草稿显示原话依据，仍是对本人自述的未确认整理。需要时修正措辞并在本机确认。
被移除、跳过或本人已审阅的条目不能被AI静默覆盖。移除针对草稿，不会删除已保存的原始
会话；原会话仍保存在本人选定的私人文件夹。草稿不能证明任何人的诚实、安全或人格。

重连同一账号和文件夹后，可继续已有会话。若想在新会话复用旧草稿，请明确选择同主题草稿，
其条目、提问次数、跳过与修正会一起沿用，但不复制原始会话。未选择时，新会话不自动检索
旧草稿。发送消息才授权把有限会话与草稿上下文交给OpenAI；查看、编辑、暂停或切换界面
语言不调用AI。

愿意时展开“再深入”，选择一个关系或人生方向。Mind、Body、Spirit、Vocation分别作为思考、
生活节奏、归属与人生方向的可选线索，不要求完成后才能匹配或加入群组。
明确点击继续聊聊时，使用当前模型与授权调用AI。

保存访谈草稿、确认私人档案陈述、发布共享摘要是独立操作。“转入私人档案审阅”只把本人
选定且审阅的措辞复制到原有私人编辑框，需要再次核对批准。不会自动进入公开摘要、好友／
群组报告或匹配分数。好友和群组受邀者继续使用简短参与流程。

这是本项目编写的方法适配，致谢HUMAN 3.0，但没有安装社区Skill、复制完整Prompt或执行
层级测评。来源许可见[致谢](docs/39_open_source_credits.md)，实际验收及独立账号／平台限制
见[当前状态](CURRENT_STATUS.md)。

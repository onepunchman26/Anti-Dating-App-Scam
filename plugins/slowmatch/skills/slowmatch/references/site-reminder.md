# Shared Site reminder / 网站统一提醒

## English

Title: **AI recommendations are a starting point for getting to know someone**

AI analysis and matching can be wrong. Profiles and statements may be incomplete or deliberately misleading. A match cannot establish someone's honesty, actual circumstances, or the safety of a relationship. Move at a pace both of you are comfortable with, keep your boundaries, and get to know each other through gradual conversation and safe, easy-to-leave meetings. Over time, consider whether their actions match their words and whether the relationship suits you. You can slow down, decline, pause, or leave at any time. You do not need to stay in a relationship that makes you uncomfortable because AI recommended it.

Short reminder: **AI can be wrong, and profiles may be untrue. Take time to get to know each other, keep your boundaries, and judge by your experience together. You can pause or leave at any time.**

Show the full reminder in the relevant homepage explanation, first joining flow, candidate detail and first shared-planning flow. The short reminder can accompany ordinary use without a forced dialog on every action. Clear threats or coercion call for timely safety support, not a mechanical suggestion to keep observing. No “AI-certified safe,” “verified honesty” or “guaranteed fit” badge may contradict this message. This reference supplies copy; only observing the actual rendered page verifies its implementation.

## 中文版

标题：**AI 推荐是认识的起点**

AI 的分析和匹配可能出错，人的资料和陈述也可能不完整，或存在刻意隐瞒与误导。匹配结果不能证明一个人的诚实程度、真实情况或关系安全。请按双方舒服的节奏，保持边界，通过逐步交流和安全、容易离开的线下接触，观察对方在一段时间里的实际行为是否与表达一致、是否真的适合你。你可以随时放慢、拒绝、暂停或退出，无须为了 AI 的推荐继续一段让你不舒服的关系。

日常简短提醒：**AI 可能误判，资料也可能不真实。慢慢了解，保留边界，以实际相处作判断；随时可以暂停或退出。**

完整提醒用于首页相关解释、首次加入、候选详情和首次共同安排流程。日常可以显示简短提醒，不必每次操作强制弹窗。有明确威胁或控制时及时提供安全支持，不能机械地要求继续观察。不使用“AI 认证安全”“诚信人格已验证”“保证适合”等相反承诺。本参考提供文案，只有实际页面可见才能证明已实现。

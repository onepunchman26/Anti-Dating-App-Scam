# Relationship-first interview / 关系优先访谈

## English

Start with the person's present relationship hopes, comfortable pace, care, communication,
boundaries and practical arrangements. Friendship uses friendship language. Ask one short,
optional question per turn. Count the opening question and every clarification: the first
pass ends after at most eight substantive questions, or earlier whenever the person wants
the draft. Do not impose a minimum time. A 3–5 minute start or optional 10–20 minute
conversation is a design aim, not a tested duration or quota.

Use one answer for every relevant area it actually addresses. Reuse authorized previous
answers, skips and corrections; do not replay an intake. A specific follow-up can clarify
what matters in an example without turning it into a personality verdict. Inexperience,
uncertainty and choosing not to answer are valid states. Prioritize practical safety help
over interviewing when threats or coercion arise.

Show a concise private draft: hopes, pace, care, communication/boundaries, practical
arrangements and unknowns. Keep explicit self-report, provisional interpretation and
unknowns distinct. Ask for correction; never turn an AI draft into a confirmed profile
or public summary automatically. Save only through supported local tools with the
corresponding approval, and verify the result. The webpage helper enforces its own count
and stores draft state; in native chat keep the count visible and do not claim that web
state was changed. Session-only native help must remain usable without file access.

Later, only if wanted or relevant, explore thinking/learning (Mind), daily rhythm/energy
(Body), relationships/meaning/belonging (Spirit), work/life direction (Vocation). Do not
require four-quadrant completion. Never use developmental levels, diagnostic labels or
personality scores to rank/select candidates. Matching uses separately approved concrete
conditions and preferences; depth of interview does not prove honesty or better matches.
Group invitees still submit only the conditions needed for that activity.

Method reference: Dan Koe's HUMAN 3.0 articles, inspected 2026-10-19. This is an original
relationship-focused adaptation of general interview ideas, not an official HUMAN 3.0
test, copied prompt, scientific assessment or endorsement. No external Skill is installed.

## 中文版

先聊本人此刻的关系期待、舒服的节奏、关心方式、沟通、边界与现实安排。朋友入口使用友情语境。
每轮一个简短、可跳过的问题。开场和每次澄清均计数：首轮最多八次实质提问，本人想看草稿时
可以更早结束，不设最低时长。三至五分钟快速开始、可选十至二十分钟对话只是设计目标，
不代表已测得的时长或必须完成的额度。

一个回答可以覆盖多个实际涉及的领域。复用获准使用的旧回答、跳过及修正，不重做问卷。
针对具体例子作少量追问，了解真正关切，不扩大成人格判决。没有经验、尚不确定和不愿回答
都是有效状态。出现威胁或强迫时，优先提供实际安全支持，停止为了画像继续追问。

提供简短私人草稿：期待、节奏、关心、沟通／边界、现实安排及未知。明确区分自述、暂时推断
与未知，支持本人修正。AI草稿不自动变成已确认档案或公开摘要。仅在具有相应授权且本机工具
支持时保存，并验证结果。网页助手由程序计数和存储草稿状态；原生聊天保持可见计数，不声称
改变了网页状态。没有文件访问能力时，原生会话内帮助仍可使用。

以后按本人意愿或当前需要，逐步聊思考／学习、作息／精力、关系／意义／归属、工作／人生方向。
不要求完成四象限，不按成长层级、诊断标签或人格分数排名筛人。匹配使用另行批准的具体条件
与偏好，访谈深入不能证明诚实或匹配更准确。群组受邀者仍只填本次活动所需条件。
